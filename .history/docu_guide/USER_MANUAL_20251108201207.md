# Introduction

## What is CacaoGuard?

CacaoGuard is an AI-powered web application designed to help cacao farmers identify and manage diseases and pests affecting their crops. The system combines machine learning disease detection with an integrated e-commerce marketplace for cacao-related products.

## System Features

### 🔍 Disease Detection
- AI-powered image analysis
- Instant disease and pest identification
- Treatment recommendations
- Scan history tracking

### 🛒 E-commerce Marketplace
- Browse and purchase cacao products
- Secure checkout with GCash or Cash on Delivery
- Order tracking and history
- Product management (Admin only)

### 🗺️ Farm Mapping
- GPS-based farm location tracking
- Multiple farm registration
- Area and tree count monitoring
- Visual farm management

### 📊 Analytics & Reporting
- Dashboard with key metrics
- Scan statistics and trends
- Order analytics
- Revenue tracking (Admin)

# Guest User Guide

## What Guests Can Do

✅ **Allowed:**
- View marketplace products (browse only)
- Perform disease detection scans (5 per day limit)
- View scan results and recommendations
- No account required

❌ **Not Allowed:**
- Purchase products
- Save scan history
- Track orders
- Access dashboard
- Register farm locations

## How to Use as Guest

### 1. Access Guest Mode

```
1. Visit homepage
2. Click "Continue as Guest" or "Guest" button
3. Automatic redirect to guest dashboard
```

### 2. View Marketplace

```
1. Click "Marketplace" in navigation
2. Browse available products
3. View product details (clicking on products)
4. Note: Cannot add to cart or purchase
```

**To Purchase:** Sign up for a user account

### 3. Perform Disease Scan

#### Step-by-Step:

**Option A: Take New Photo**
```
1. Click "Disease Detection" or scan icon
2. Click "Take Photo" or camera button
3. Allow camera access when prompted
4. Position cacao leaf/plant in frame
5. Ensure good lighting
6. Click capture button
7. Review photo
8. Click "Analyze" or "Submit"
9. Wait for AI analysis (5-10 seconds)
10. View results
```

**Option B: Upload Existing Photo**
```
1. Click "Disease Detection"
2. Click "Upload Image" or upload icon
3. Select photo from device
4. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, JPEG
5. Click "Analyze"
6. Wait for results
```

### 4. Understanding Scan Results

**Result Display:**
- **Disease/Pest Name:** Identified condition
- **Confidence Level:** AI certainty (0-100%)
- **Severity:** Low, Medium, High
- **Symptoms:** What to look for
- **Treatment Recommendations:** How to treat
- **Prevention Tips:** How to prevent spread

**Confidence Level Guide:**
- **80-100%:** High confidence, reliable result
- **60-79%:** Moderate confidence, likely accurate
- **Below 60%:** Low confidence, consider retaking photo

### 5. Daily Scan Limit

**Limit:** 5 scans per day for guests

**When limit reached:**
- Message: "Daily scan limit reached"
- Options: 
  - Sign up for unlimited scans
  - Wait until next day (resets at midnight)

**Tip:** Create an account for unlimited free scans!

### 6. Create Account (Upgrade from Guest)

```
1. Click "Sign Up" button
2. Fill in required information:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - Address
   - Password (min 8 characters)
   - Confirm Password
3. Select "User" role (Farmer)
4. Click "Register"
5. Verify email if required
6. Login with new credentials
```

**Benefits of Upgrading:**
- ✅ Unlimited disease scans
- ✅ Purchase products
- ✅ Track orders
- ✅ Save scan history
- ✅ Register farm locations
- ✅ Access dashboard

## Disease Detection & Scanning

### Performing a Scan

#### Method 1: Camera Capture
```
1. Navigate to "Disease Detection" page
2. Click "Take Photo" button
3. Allow camera access
4. Position leaf/plant in frame:
   - Good lighting (natural light best)
   - Clear focus
   - Fill frame with leaf
   - Avoid shadows
5. Click capture
6. Review image quality
7. Click "Analyze Image"
8. Wait for results (5-10 seconds)
```

#### Method 2: Upload Photo
```
1. Go to "Disease Detection"
2. Click "Upload Image"
3. Select photo from device
4. Ensure photo quality:
   - High resolution
   - Clear focus
   - Good lighting
5. Click "Analyze"
6. View results
```

### Best Practices for Scanning

✅ **DO:**
- Use natural daylight
- Focus on affected areas
- Take multiple photos of different leaves
- Use high-resolution images
- Keep leaf centered in frame

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use flash (can distort colors)
- Scan in dim lighting
- Include multiple leaves in one scan
- Use blurry images
- Scan healthy leaves (for disease detection)

### Understanding Scan Results

**Result Information:**
- **Disease/Pest Identified:** Name and type
- **Confidence Score:** AI certainty percentage
- **Severity Level:** 
  - 🟢 Low: Minor infection, easy to treat
  - 🟡 Medium: Moderate spread, requires attention
  - 🔴 High: Severe infection, urgent action needed

**Detailed Information:**
- **Symptoms Description:** Visual signs to look for
- **Causes:** How infection occurs
- **Treatment Recommendations:** 
  - Organic solutions
  - Chemical treatments
  - Cultural practices
- **Prevention Measures:** How to avoid future infections
- **Related Products:** Recommended treatments from marketplace

# FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

## General Questions

**Q: Is CacaoGuard free to use?**
A: Yes! Registration and disease detection are completely free. You only pay for products you purchase from the marketplace.

**Q: What devices can I use?**
A: CacaoGuard works on desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones. Any device with a modern web browser and internet connection.

**Q: Do I need to download an app?**
A: No, CacaoGuard is a web application. Access it directly through your browser at https://cacaoguard2.onrender.com.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure Firebase authentication.

## Account Questions

**Q: Difference between Guest and User account?**
A:
- **Guest:** Limited to 5 scans/day, cannot purchase products
- **User:** Unlimited scans, can shop, track orders, register farms

**Q: How do I upgrade from Guest to User?**
A: Click "Sign Up", create an account, and login. Your guest session data won't transfer.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes, go to Profile Settings and update your email. Verify the new email address.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact admin to request account deletion. Note: This is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Scanning Questions

**Q: How accurate is the AI detection?**
A: Accuracy depends on image quality. Confidence scores above 80% are generally reliable. Always consult an expert for confirmation.

**Q: What diseases can be detected?**
A: The system detects common cacao diseases including Black Pod, Witches' Broom, Frosty Pod Rot, and various pest infestations.

**Q: Can I scan multiple leaves at once?**
A: For best results, scan one leaf or affected area per image.

**Q: How long does analysis take?**
A: Typically 5-10 seconds depending on image size and internet speed.

**Q: Can I save scan results?**
A: Yes, if you're logged in as a User. Scan history is saved automatically.

## Shopping Questions

**Q: Is delivery available nationwide?**
A: Check with admin for current delivery areas. Coverage may vary.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Currently GCash (online payment) and Cash on Delivery (COD).

**Q: How long until my order is delivered?**
A: Delivery time varies by location. Typically 3-7 business days after order confirmation.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Yes, if the order status is still "Pending". Contact admin for confirmed orders.

**Q: What if I receive a damaged product?**
A: Contact admin immediately with photos. We'll arrange replacement or refund.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**
A: Yes, for valid reasons (wrong item, damaged goods, etc.). Refunds processed after verification.

## Farm Location Questions

**Q: Why register farm locations?**
A: Track multiple farms, monitor total area, count trees, and organize your farming operations.

**Q: Can I register multiple farms?**
A: Yes, unlimited farm registrations per user account.

**Q: Is GPS location accurate?**
A: GPS accuracy varies (5-10 meters typical). Use outdoors for best results.

**Q: Can I change farm coordinates?**
A: No, coordinates are fixed upon creation. Delete and recreate if needed.

## Technical Questions

**Q: Why is the site slow?**
A: Could be due to internet speed, many simultaneous users, or server maintenance. Try during off-peak hours.

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions). Mobile browsers also supported.

**Q: Do I need fast internet?**
A: Minimum 2 Mbps recommended. Faster speeds improve image upload and analysis time.

**Q: Can I use offline?**
A: No, internet connection required for all features (AI analysis, database access, image hosting).
