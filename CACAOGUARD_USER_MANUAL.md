## Introduction

### What is CacaoGuard?
CacaoGuard is an intelligent web-based platform designed specifically for cacao farmers and agricultural professionals. The system combines AI-powered disease and pest detection with an integrated e-commerce marketplace for cacao products, providing a comprehensive solution for cacao farm management.

### Key Benefits
- **Early Disease Detection**: Identify cacao diseases and pests before they spread
- **Expert Recommendations**: Get treatment suggestions for detected issues
- **Market Access**: Buy and sell cacao products directly
- **Farm Tracking**: Monitor multiple farm locations on interactive maps
- **Order Management**: Track purchases and sales in one place
- **Data History**: Keep records of all scans and farm activities

### System Requirements
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions)
- **Internet Connection**: Required for all features
- **Device**: Desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone
- **Image Format**: JPEG or PNG for disease scanning

## Disease & Pest Detection

### Disease Detection System

#### Supported Diseases
The system can detect the following cacao diseases:

1. **Black Pod Rot Disease**
   - Caused by Phytophthora fungi
   - Most common cacao disease
   - Can cause 30-90% yield loss
   - **Symptoms**: Dark brown/black spots on pods

2. **Fito Disease (Phytophthora Root Rot)**
   - Attacks root system and trunk
   - Causes wilting and yellowing
   - Thrives in waterlogged soil
   - **Symptoms**: Bark lesions, dark fluid oozing

3. **Monilia Disease**
   - Affects pods at all development stages
   - Causes internal rot
   - **Symptoms**: Irregular swellings, cream-colored powder

4. **Healthy Plants**
   - No disease detected
   - Continue current practices

5. **Unknown Data**
   - Image unclear or unusual symptoms
   - Requires expert consultation

#### How to Perform Disease Scan

**Step 1: Prepare Your Image**
- Take a clear, well-lit photo of the affected area
- Focus on symptoms (spots, discoloration, lesions)
- Avoid blurry or dark images
- Recommended distance: 15-30cm from plant
- File format: JPEG or PNG
- Maximum file size: 5MB

**Step 2: Upload Image**
1. Go to **"Scan & Diagnose"** in the main menu
2. Select **"Disease Detection"** card
3. Click **"Choose Disease Image"** or drag & drop
4. Wait for image preview to appear
5. System automatically starts analysis

**Step 3: View Results**
- **Detection Result**: Disease name identified
- **Confidence Level**: Accuracy percentage (0-100%)
  - High (80-100%): Very reliable
  - Medium (60-80%): Likely accurate
  - Low (0-60%): Requires verification
- **Recommendations**: Treatment suggestions provided
- **Action Items**: Steps to take immediately

**Step 4: Save or Act**
- Results automatically saved to your scan history
- Download report (if needed)
- Share with agricultural expert
- Implement recommended treatments

### Pest Detection System

#### Supported Pests

1. **Ant Weaver**
   - Protects other pests (aphids, mealybugs)
   - Constructs nests with leaves
   - Attacks natural predators
   - **Symptoms**: Ant trails, leaf nests

2. **Aphids**
   - Sucks sap from shoots and leaves
   - Causes leaf curling and yellowing
   - Reproduces very rapidly
   - **Symptoms**: Sticky honeydew, sooty mold

3. **Mealybug**
   - Covered with white waxy powder
   - Causes wilting and yellowing
   - Can reduce pod production by 50%
   - **Symptoms**: Cotton-like masses on plants

4. **Healthy Plants**
   - No pest infestation detected

5. **Unknown Data**
   - Unable to identify pest
   - May require physical specimen examination

#### How to Perform Pest Scan

**Step 1: Capture Pest Evidence**
- Take close-up photo showing the pest
- Include damage symptoms if pest not visible
- Ensure good lighting and focus
- Show both pest and affected plant parts

**Step 2: Upload for Analysis**
1. Navigate to **"Scan & Diagnose"**
2. Select **"Pest Detection"** card
3. Upload image via button or drag-and-drop
4. Wait for analysis (typically 5-10 seconds)

**Step 3: Review Findings**
- **Pest Identified**: Species name
- **Confidence Score**: Detection accuracy
- **Damage Assessment**: Severity level
- **Treatment Plan**: Control recommendations

**Step 4: Take Action**
- Follow integrated pest management (IPM) guidelines
- Apply recommended treatments
- Monitor population levels
- Track results in your scan history

### Understanding Recommendations

#### Treatment Recommendations Include:
- **Immediate Actions**: What to do right now
- **Chemical Control**: Approved pesticides/fungicides (if needed)
- **Biological Control**: Natural predators, beneficial insects
- **Cultural Practices**: Farming methods to prevent recurrence
- **Monitoring Schedule**: When to check again

#### Recommendation Priority Levels:
- 🔴 **URGENT**: Act within 24 hours
- 🟡 **IMPORTANT**: Act within 1 week
- 🟢 **PREVENTIVE**: General maintenance

### Scan History

   - Scan type (disease/pest)

- **Delete Scan**: Remove from history (if needed)
- **Re-analyze**: Upload same image again

1. **Cash on Delivery (COD)**

### Order Tracking

#### Order Status Flow
Your order progresses through these stages:

1. **Pending** 🟡
   - Order received, awaiting admin confirmation
   - Payment verification (if applicable)
   - Typical duration: 1-24 hours

2. **Confirmed** 🔵
   - Order approved by seller
   - Payment validated
   - Being prepared for shipment
   - Typical duration: 1-2 days

3. **Shipped** 🟣
   - Package handed to courier
   - Tracking number provided (if available)
   - In transit to your location
   - Typical duration: 2-7 days

4. **Delivered** 🟢
   - Package successfully received
   - Order complete
   - You can now leave a review

5. **Cancelled** 🔴
   - Order cancelled by you or seller
   - Refund processed (if payment made)

#### How to Check Order Status
1. Go to **"My Orders"** in your account
2. View all orders listed by date
3. Click order number for details
4. See current status and timeline
5. Contact seller if issues arise

#### Order Details Include
- **Order Number**: Unique identifier
- **Order Date**: When placed
- **Items Ordered**: Product list with quantities
- **Total Amount**: Final payment
- **Shipping Address**: Delivery location
- **Payment Method**: How you're paying
- **Status Updates**: Timeline with timestamps
- **Courier Information**: Tracking details (when shipped)

## Farm Mapping

### Overview
The Farm Mapping feature allows you to mark, track, and manage multiple cacao farm locations on an interactive map.

### Adding a Farm Location

#### Step 1: Access Farm Mapping
1. Click **"Farm Mapping"** in navigation
2. Interactive map loads (powered by Leaflet/Mapbox)
3. Default view shows Oriental Mindoro region

#### Step 2: Add New Farm
1. Click **"Add Farm"** button
2. Fill in farm information:
   - **Farm Name**: Descriptive name
   - **Municipality**: Select from dropdown
   - **Barangay**: Specific location
   - **Area**: Total hectares
   - **Number of Trees**: Cacao tree count
   - **Farm Status**: Active/Inactive
   - **Contact Person**: Farm manager name
   - **Contact Number**: Phone for this farm
   - **Description**: Additional details

#### Step 3: Set Location on Map
1. Click on the map where farm is located
2. Pin marker drops at that location
3. Latitude and longitude auto-filled
4. Adjust marker by dragging (if needed)

#### Step 4: Upload Farm Images
1. Click **"Choose Images"** button
2. Select one or multiple photos
3. Supported: JPEG, PNG (up to 5MB each)
4. Images upload to Cloudinary
5. Thumbnails appear in preview
6. Remove unwanted images with X button

#### Step 5: Save Farm
1. Review all information
2. Click **"Save Farm"** button
3. Farm added to Firestore database
4. Pin appears on map with farm name
5. Farm listed in your farms table

### Viewing Farm Locations

#### Map Features
- **Zoom Controls**: +/- buttons or mouse wheel
- **Pan**: Click and drag to move map
- **Cluster Markers**: Multiple farms shown as numbered clusters
- **Click Marker**: View farm details popup
- **Filter Farms**: By municipality or status
- **Search**: Find specific farm by name

#### Farm Information Popup
Clicking a farm marker shows:
- Farm name and location
- Total area and tree count
- Farm status
- Image gallery (if photos uploaded)
- Edit and delete buttons (for your farms)
- View full details link

### Editing Farm Information

#### How to Edit
1. Click farm marker on map
2. Click **"Edit"** button in popup
3. Or use **"My Farms"** list and click edit icon
4. Update any field:
   - Name, location, area, trees
   - Contact information
   - Status (active/inactive)
   - Description
5. Add or remove images
6. Move map marker if location changed
7. Click **"Update Farm"** to save

#### What Can Be Edited
- ✅ All text information
- ✅ Farm images (add/remove)
- ✅ Map coordinates
- ✅ Status and visibility
- ❌ Cannot change creation date
- ❌ Cannot change farm ID

### Deleting a Farm

#### Step-by-Step Deletion
1. Locate farm on map or in list
2. Click **"Delete"** button
3. Confirmation dialog appears:
   - "Are you sure you want to delete this farm?"
   - This action cannot be undone
4. Click **"Confirm"** to delete
5. Farm removed from database
6. Marker disappears from map
7. Images deleted from Cloudinary

**⚠️ Warning**: Deletion is permanent and includes all associated scan data!

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### Login Problems

**Issue: "Invalid credentials" error**
- ✅ Check email spelling (no typos)
- ✅ Verify password (case-sensitive)
- ✅ Try "Forgot Password" to reset
- ✅ Clear browser cache and cookies
- ✅ Try different browser

**Issue: "Account not found"**
- ✅ Verify you registered with this email
- ✅ Check spam folder for verification email
- ✅ Contact admin if account deleted

**Issue: Can't receive password reset email**
- ✅ Check spam/junk folder
- ✅ Verify email address is correct

### Disease Reference Guide

#### Black Pod Rot
- **Scientific Name**: Phytophthora palmivora/megakarya
- **Symptoms**: Black/brown lesions on pods, white mycelium
- **Spread**: Water splash, high humidity
- **Peak Season**: Rainy season
- **Management**: Fungicide, sanitation, drainage

#### Fito Disease (Root Rot)
- **Scientific Name**: Phytophthora spp.
- **Symptoms**: Wilting, yellowing, bark cankers
- **Spread**: Waterlogged soil
- **Peak Season**: Wet season
- **Management**: Improve drainage, resistant varieties

#### Monilia Disease
- **Scientific Name**: Moniliophthora roreri
- **Symptoms**: Pod swellings, cream powder, internal rot
- **Spread**: Wind, rain splash
- **Peak Season**: Humid months
- **Management**: Sanitation, fungicides, regular harvest

### Pest Reference Guide

#### Weaver Ants
- **Scientific Name**: Oecophylla spp.
- **Behavior**: Protect aphids/mealybugs for honeydew
- **Damage**: Indirect - increases pest populations
- **Management**: Destroy nests, ant baits, prune branches

#### Aphids
- **Family**: Aphididae
- **Damage**: Sap-sucking, leaf curl, sooty mold
- **Reproduction**: Very rapid (asexual)
- **Management**: Natural predators, insecticidal soap, neem oil

#### Mealybugs
- **Family**: Pseudococcidae
- **Appearance**: White waxy coating
- **Damage**: Sap-sucking, plant weakening
- **Management**: Systemic insecticides, biological control
